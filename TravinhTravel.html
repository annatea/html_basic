Introduction

Tra Vinh is a province in the Mekong Delta, famous for its unique Khmer culture and many attractive tourist destinations.

When visiting Tra Vinh, tourists can explore Ao Ba Om pond, Ba Dong Beach, ancient Khmer temples, and many historical sites that reflect the local identity.

1.The Ba Om pond

📍 Ward 8, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Ao Ba Om is one of the most iconic landmarks in Tra Vinh, attracting visitors with its tranquil lake, centuries-old trees, and serene surroundings. It is not only a beautiful natural destination but also a place rich in fascinating Khmer folklore and cultural heritage.

Ao Ba Om is especially attractive for its spacious and tranquil surroundings, making it a perfect destination for leisurely walks, photography, and relaxation. During the annual Ok Om Bok Festival, the site comes alive with colorful traditional cultural events and celebrations.

2.Ba Dong beach

📍 Truong Long Hoa Commune, Duyen Hai Town, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Ba Dong Beach is one of Tra Vinh’s most popular seaside destinations, offering a wide, open coastline and a peaceful atmosphere. With its unspoiled beauty and relaxing environment, it is an excellent choice for travelers seeking a quiet escape from the busy city life.

Visitors can enjoy swimming, camping, admiring the wind turbines, and tasting fresh seafood. The beach is at its most beautiful in the early morning and late afternoon, when the weather is cool and pleasant, creating perfect conditions for photography.

3.The Con Chim islet

📍 Cu Lao Con Chim Hamlet, Hoa Minh Commune, Chau Thanh District, Vinh Long Province, Vietnam.

Con Chim Islet is one of Tra Vinh’s most popular ecotourism destinations, renowned for its charming river landscapes and refreshing natural environment. Preserving the authentic beauty of the countryside, it offers a peaceful retreat for visitors seeking relaxation and a closer connection to nature.

What makes Con Chim Islet unique is its eco-friendly tourism model, which combines sustainable farming with environmental conservation. Visitors can stroll or cycle along quiet paths, admire the lush rice fields, experience authentic rural life, and embrace the relaxed pace of this charming river island.

Ho Chi Minh Memorial Temple

📍 Ward 8, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

The Ho Chi Minh Memorial Temple is one of Tra Vinh’s significant historical landmarks, built to honor the legacy of President Ho Chi Minh and express the gratitude of local residents. With its serene surroundings, beautiful landscape, and solemn atmosphere, it is a meaningful destination for visitors interested in history and culture.

Surrounded by lush greenery and tranquil ponds, the temple complex offers spacious grounds and informative exhibits that provide insight into its history and significance. It is a perfect destination for sightseeing, reflection, and learning in a calm and peaceful atmosphere.

5.Tra Vinh University

📍 No. 126 Nguyen Thien Thanh Street, Ward 5, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Tra Vinh University is one of the most prominent educational institutions in the province, known for its spacious, modern, and beautifully landscaped campus. Beyond its academic role, the university has become a popular spot for visitors seeking a pleasant environment for sightseeing and photography.

With its vibrant student life, academic activities, and cultural events, the university reflects the youthful and dynamic spirit of Tra Vinh. It is an excellent place to explore a modern side of the province while enjoying a lively and inspiring atmosphere.

6.Ang khmer Pagoda (Wat Ang)

📍 Ao Ba Om Cultural Site, Ward 8, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Ang Pagoda is one of Tra Vinh’s most famous Khmer temples, admired for its elegant architecture, vibrant yet harmonious colors, and peaceful surroundings. The pagoda serves as an important cultural and spiritual center, showcasing the rich traditions and heritage of the Khmer people in Southern Vietnam.

A distinctive feature of Ang Pagoda is its traditional Khmer architecture, shaded grounds, and major religious festivals held throughout the year, such as Chol Chnam Thmay, Dolta, and Ok Om Bok. By visiting the pagoda, tourists can not only admire its beauty but also gain a deeper understanding of the local spiritual and cultural traditions.

7.Hang Pagoda

📍 Ward 8, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Hang Pagoda is one of the most famous Khmer pagodas in Tra Vinh. It is well known for its unique cave-shaped entrance gate, spacious grounds, and peaceful atmosphere surrounded by lush greenery.

The pagoda is also known for its bird sanctuary and rich cultural and religious heritage. Visitors can enjoy sightseeing, photography, and the peaceful atmosphere of this distinctive Khmer pagoda in Tra Vinh.

8. Huynh Kha Tourist Area, Tra Vinh

📍 Ward 9, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

Huynh Kha Tourist Area is a popular destination for recreation and relaxation in Tra Vinh. With a wide range of facilities and activities, it is an ideal place for families, friends, and large groups to enjoy their time together.

With its cafes, restaurants, karaoke facilities, entertainment areas, and relaxing green spaces, Huynh Kha is a great place to enjoy good food, have fun, and take memorable photos during a short visit.

9.Khmer Ethnic Culture Museum

📍 National Highway 53, Group 4, Ward 8, Tra Vinh City, Tra Vinh Province, Vietnam.

The Khmer Ethnic Culture Museum is a place where visitors can explore the rich cultural heritage of the Khmer community in Southern Vietnam. With its beautiful architecture, lush green surroundings, and open atmosphere, it is an attractive destination for cultural and educational visits.

Inside the museum, there are many exhibition rooms about Khmer pagodas, daily life, traditional craft villages, and folk musical instruments. This is a very suitable destination for those who want to better understand the history, art, and cultural identity of the Khmer people in Tra Vinh.
